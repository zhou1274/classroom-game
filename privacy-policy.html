Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

What This Policy Covers

ClassroomGames is a browser game site built for quick play at school. This policy explains what the site and its hosting provider may know when you open a page, and how advertising partners may use data when ads are shown.

Information We Collect

We do not ask for an account, email address, password, or payment details to play a game. The pages are static, so there is no user profile stored on the site itself.

Like most websites, the hosting provider may receive standard server logs, including the requested page, browser type, device type, language, approximate region, and IP address. Those logs are used for security, monitoring, and troubleshooting.

Cookies and Advertising

The site may use cookies or similar technologies to remember basic preferences and to measure how visitors use the pages. If Google AdSense ads are enabled, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies to serve and measure ads, including interest-based advertising where allowed by law.

Google's ad systems may collect limited data such as your device, browser, and general location. You can learn about Google's advertising practices and available controls in Google's Privacy & Terms page and Ads Settings.

Embedded Game Providers

The game area embeds game content from third-party platforms. When a game loads, that provider may set its own cookies or collect technical data under its own privacy policy. ClassroomGames does not control the content, cookies, or data practices inside those frames.

How We Use Information

Information is used to keep the site available, understand broad traffic patterns, prevent abuse, and support a safe browsing experience. We do not sell personal information and do not use the game pages to collect names, contacts, or school records.

Children and School Use

The site is a general audience service and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Teachers and parents should review the game before class and decide whether the experience fits their students.

Your Choices

You can clear cookies, block third-party cookies, or disable personalized advertising in your browser. You can also close the game page if it asks for personal details or unusual permissions.

Changes and Contact

We may update this policy when the site, advertising setup, or legal requirements change. Questions can be sent to contact@classroom-game.com.
