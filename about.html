About ClassroomGames

Last updated: August 28, 2026

ClassroomGames is an independent browser game library for quick, school-friendly breaks.

Why ClassroomGames Exists

ClassroomGames is a free browser game library for students and teachers. The goal is to make a school-friendly game easy to find, easy to open, and easy to stop when class begins again.

Who It Is For

Students get a short mental break between lessons. Teachers get a quiet choice activity, a discussion prompt, or a simple way to test a game before recommending it. Every page includes controls, device notes, and classroom tips.

How Games Are Presented

Most games are embedded from external platforms that provide browser play. The site does not require a download, account, or purchase for the current experience. Game ownership remains with the original creator or publisher.

Our Editorial Approach

Pages are written for real classroom use: short paragraphs, clear controls, practical safety notes, and no misleading claims. We do not promise that a game will bypass a school network.

Contact

Ideas, corrections, and broken-game reports are welcome at 623837699@qq.com. Please include the game name and the device or browser you were using.
