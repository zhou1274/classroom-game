    How to Play Bloons Tower Defense Unblocked

    Place towers along the path, earn money from popped balloons, and use upgrades to improve range or damage. Keep enough coverage near the exit where balloons are hardest to stop.

    A good first session does not need a perfect score. Start with one short round, learn where the controls are, and then decide whether to try a higher goal in the next attempt. This keeps the game easy to stop and gives every student a fair way to measure progress.

    Desktop Controls

    Place a tower on the map and use the mouse to select a target. Upgrade towers when you have enough money to stop the next wave.

    Mobile Controls

    Tap a tower from the menu and tap the map location where you want it. Use the upgrade buttons during the round.

    Classroom Tips

    Cover the strongest towers near bends in the path. Balloons spend more time in range there, which gives the upgrades more chances to hit.
