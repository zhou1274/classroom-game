    How to Play Checkers Unblocked

    Move one piece diagonally to an empty square. Jump over an opponent piece to capture it, and continue jumping when another capture is available.

    A good first session does not need a perfect score. Start with one short round, learn where the controls are, and then decide whether to try a higher goal in the next attempt. This keeps the game easy to stop and gives every student a fair way to measure progress.

    Desktop Controls

    Click or tap a piece and then choose the destination square. A jump is taken automatically when the move is valid.

    Mobile Controls

    Tap the piece and then tap the empty square where you want to land.

    Classroom Tips

    Before moving, count how many jumps the opponent can make next turn. Defending the board is often more important than attacking one piece.
