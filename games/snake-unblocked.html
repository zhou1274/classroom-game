    Play Snake Unblocked at School

    Snake Unblocked is a free browser version of the classic snake game. You control a small snake that moves around a square board. Eat the food, grow longer, and try to beat your best score before class starts again.

    Everything runs directly in the page. There is no download, no account, and no setup. You can open it on a Chromebook, laptop, tablet, or phone with a modern browser. If your school network allows this site, the game should load in a few seconds.

    Why Snake Is a Good Classroom Game

    Snake is easy to explain, quick to start, and easy to pause. A round can last under a minute or continue for a few minutes, so it fits well between lessons, during a short break, or as a quiet reward activity.

    For Students

    You do not need to read a long tutorial. Move in one direction, avoid a wall, and do not run into yourself. The controls are simple enough that you can start playing immediately, and the game becomes more challenging as the snake grows longer.

    For Teachers

    Snake is a low-friction choice for supervised free time. It has short rounds, clear rules, and no personal data request. Teachers can set a time limit, ask students to play a specific number of rounds, or use the game as a quick movement and focus activity.

    How to Play Snake Unblocked

    The game starts when you press New Game. Move the snake around the board and eat the red food. Each piece of food increases your score and makes the snake longer. The game ends when the snake hits a wall or its own body.

    Desktop Controls

    Use the arrow keys or WASD to change direction. Press SPACE to pause and ESC to restart. You can also choose Easy, Normal, Hard, or Insane difficulty before starting a new game.

    Mobile Controls

    Swipe in any direction to move the snake. On a phone, the on-screen arrow buttons also appear under the game board. Use the Pause button when you need to stop quickly.

    Is Snake Unblocked Safe at School?

    Safe to Play

    This is a simple browser game with no download, no sign-up, and no request for personal information. The game stores your high score in local browser storage, so it stays on your own device.

    School Network Rules

    This site does not bypass school filters or network policies. Whether a game is available depends on the device, browser, and school network rules. If a page asks for money, private details, or unusual permissions, close it and ask a teacher.

    FAQ About Snake Unblocked

    How do I play Snake Unblocked?

    Use the arrow keys or WASD on a computer, or swipe on a phone, to move the snake. Eat food to grow and avoid the walls and your own body. Press the pause button when class starts again.

    Is Snake Unblocked safe for school?

    This game runs locally in the browser, has no download or account requirement, and does not ask for personal information. Teachers should still review the game and follow their school internet rules.

    Can I play Snake Unblocked on a Chromebook or phone?

    Yes. The game works in a modern browser on a Chromebook, laptop, tablet, or phone. Touch controls appear on smaller screens and keyboard controls work on a computer.

    Does Snake Unblocked save my score?

    The game keeps a high score in your browser's local storage. You do not need an account, and the score stays on your own device.

    Teacher tip: If you use Snake as a short classroom activity, set a round limit before students begin. A time limit keeps the game focused, gives every student the same challenge, and makes it easier to return to learning when the timer ends.
